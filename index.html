PowerPod Platform™

Why is a standard needed?

The same power system gets designed a hundred times over, and no two of them fit together.

Every incompatible connector, protocol and form factor is duplicated effort, paid for once by the engineers who build it, and again by everyone who has to design around it.

One form factor.

Designed for portability.

Larger loses portability. Smaller loses utility. This is the balance.

Standard outside. Agnostic inside.

The envelope is fixed. What fills it is not.

One published mechanical and electrical shell, so any compliant pod fits any compliant bay.

Use the chemistry you trust.

NMC, LFP, whatever you qualify.

Cells stand or lie however your pack wants them. The outside never changes.

Integrate the BMS you believe in.

The board is yours. The station it mounts to is ours.

Mounting, clearance and sense wiring are published, so the electronics can change without redrawing the pack.

Designed once. Compatible everywhere.

One connector face. Power, data and guidance pins.

Drawn once, so nobody has to draw it again.

Every PowerPod, born connected.

Telematics is not a line item.

Cell voltages, temperature, position and state of health leave the pack from the first minute it exists.

PowerPodOS

One place where every pod lives.

Fleet, health, throughput and revenue in a single system of record. One dataset for the whole platform, not one per vendor.

PowerPod App

The OEM's app, without the OEM building one.

Swap, charge, pay and monitor. A canvas tuned per OEM, running on the same data as the PowerPodOS.

No network. Still a business.

The pod talks to the phone over BLE.

Sessions authorise, meter and bill on the handset, then settle when a network returns. Off grid is a supported mode, not an outage.
