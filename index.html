PowerPod Platform™

Why is a standard needed?

The same power system gets designed a hundred times over, and no two of them fit together.

Every incompatible connector, protocol and form factor is duplicated effort — paid for once by the engineers who build it, and again by everyone who has to design around it.

Hardware

One mechanical and electrical envelope, published in full.

Defined once, so that any compliant pod fits any compliant bay.

  • Form factor
  • Connector
  • Thermal limits
  • Safety margins

Software

A common control surface across every compliant device.

One protocol for discovery, telemetry and control, with reference implementations and a conformance suite — so integration is a known quantity rather than a project.
